# Medical Affairs Evidence Response SOP

> **SYNTHETIC DEMO CONTENT — NOT A REAL COMPANY SOP OR MEDICAL GUIDANCE**

**Document ID:** DOC-MA-SOP-001  
**Status:** APPROVED synthetic operating policy for demo use

## Purpose
Define how the fictional Medical Affairs Decision Intelligence workflow should retrieve, synthesize, and communicate scientific evidence.

## Approved evidence hierarchy
1. APPROVED product-information demo documents.
2. APPROVED clinical study summaries and publication-style abstracts.
3. APPROVED Medical Information responses and scientific FAQs.
4. DRAFT or EXPIRED documents may be surfaced only when explicitly requested and must be clearly labeled as non-approved; they must not support an approved scientific conclusion.

## Response requirements
Every substantive scientific response should:
- distinguish retrieved facts from AI synthesis;
- identify the relevant study or document source;
- avoid patient-specific diagnosis or treatment recommendations;
- avoid unsupported cross-study superiority claims;
- state when evidence is insufficient;
- preserve approval-status context.

## Escalation rule
If a user requests a conclusion that is not supported by the APPROVED staged evidence set, respond that sufficient approved evidence is unavailable and recommend review by the appropriate Medical Affairs subject-matter expert.

## Demo objective
The system should demonstrate that semantic retrieval alone is not sufficient: governance metadata and evidence status must shape what the agent is allowed to synthesize into a response.
